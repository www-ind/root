Our Approach

Two specialists.
One unhurried standard of care.

"We built Kanha Clinic around a simple idea — that a family shouldn't need three different waiting rooms to be looked after well."

From newborn checkups to adult cardiac screening, every consultation at Kanha Clinic is unhurried, thorough, and explained in plain language — never rushed, never generic.

Dr. Pratik Patel brings fellowship-level training in echocardiography and structured diabetes management, while Dr. Khushbu Patel focuses solely on the health of children — from infancy through adolescence.

Areas of Care

What we treat, under one roof.

01

General & Internal Medicine

Diagnosis and ongoing management of everyday and chronic adult illness, with a doctor who tracks your history over time.

02

Echocardiography & Cardiac Screening

Fellowship-trained cardiac imaging for early detection and monitoring of heart conditions, read and explained by Dr. Pratik Patel.

03

Diabetes Management

Structured, certified (CPCDM) diabetes care — from initial diagnosis to long-term insulin and lifestyle management plans.

04

Paediatric & Newborn Care

Complete infant and child healthcare from Dr. Khushbu Patel — routine visits, illness care, and developmental monitoring.

05

Vaccination & Growth Tracking

On-schedule immunisation and growth-chart monitoring, so every milestone is recorded and never missed.

06

Preventive Health Checkups

Full-family screening packages designed to catch concerns early, for both adults and children.

07

Geriatric Care

Comprehensive health management tailored specifically to the needs of the elderly to improve quality of life.

08

Asthma & Allergy Care

Diagnostic evaluation and long-term care plans for chronic allergies, asthma, and respiratory conditions.

09

Diet & Nutrition Counseling

Personalized advice and meal planning to complement treatment protocols for diabetes and cardiovascular disease.

10

Minor Surgical Procedures

Basic, safe, in-clinic procedures including wound care, suturing, and abscess drainage without hospital visits.

11

Women's Health Follow-up

Regular assessment and ongoing support for women’s health concerns, from routine screening to preventive counselling.

12

Lifestyle & Wellness Counseling

Practical guidance on diet, rest, exercise, stress, and daily habits that supports long-term health improvements.

    Health Tools

    Six quick checks, from the same guidelines
    our doctors use in the room.

    These tools give general, educational estimates only — they run entirely in your browser and nothing is stored or sent anywhere. They are not a diagnosis. For a personal assessment, please visit Dr. Pratik Patel or Dr. Khushbu Patel at Kanha Clinic.
    Uses Asian-population BMI cut-offs (commonly applied in Indian clinical practice), which are stricter than the standard WHO scale.
    Based on the Indian Diabetes Risk Score (IDRS) screening model widely used in Indian primary care. A high score means screening blood tests are worth doing — not that you have diabetes.
    If resting heart rate is left blank, zones are calculated as a simple percentage of estimated maximum heart rate (220 − age). With resting heart rate, the more precise Karvonen method is used.
    Compares your child's measurements to approximate WHO median growth values. This is indicative only — a full percentile assessment needs Dr. Khushbu Patel's complete growth chart.
    Reference schedule follows India's national immunisation guidelines. Please confirm exact timing and any catch-up doses with Dr. Khushbu Patel.
    Categories follow standard adult blood pressure guidelines. A single reading is not conclusive — blood pressure should be checked on more than one occasion.
